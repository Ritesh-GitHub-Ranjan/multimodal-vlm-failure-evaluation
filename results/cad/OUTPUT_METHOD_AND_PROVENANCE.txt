THESIS FINAL OUTPUTS V1

CAD uses the corrected 60-row summary in cad_finalisation_v1.
Values and intervals are expressed in percentage points.
The 95% intervals are paired question-level percentile bootstrap intervals
(10,000 resamples; seed 20261029). Point estimates were reconstructed from
raw model files; intervals were attached from the existing bootstrap output.
The bootstrap was not independently rerun during finalisation.

CAD is a condition-level difference between accuracy deterioration and
confidence-proxy deterioration, not an individual calibration score.

Proxy-ECE uses ECE v1, primary_exclude_empty, ten equal-width bins.
Empty predictions and rows without valid confidence are excluded.
It compares a token-confidence proxy with VQA soft scores and is not
conventional answer-level expected calibration error. It is descriptive.

SVG figures are vector graphics and can be scaled without raster blur.
Old thesis_tables_v2 and thesis_figures_v3 outputs remain unchanged.
